झन्डा बुर्जा

Jhanda Burja · Tihar street dice
  1. Six dice, six symbols: झन्डा, बुर्जा, इँट, पान, सुरत, चिडी.
  2. Bet on symbols. The host shakes the dice in a steel tumbler and lifts it.
  3. Your symbol on 2 or more dice: you get your bet back plus your bet × the number of dice. On 0 or 1 die, the house takes it.
  4. 15 rolls in an evening. Or swap places and be the house yourself.

सेलरोटीको खेल · Play money only. We play with sel roti, never real money.

कसरी खेल्ने?

How to play
🎲

The dice and the mat

Each of the 6 dice has the same six symbols: झन्डा (flag), बुर्जा (crown), इँट (diamond), पान (heart), सुरत (spade) and चिडी (club). The mat has one square for each symbol.

👆

Placing bets (दाउ लगाऔं)

Tap a symbol on the mat to bet 1 sel roti on it. Tap again for more, and bet on as many symbols as you like. ↺ फिर्ता takes your bets back. Then press हान · Roll.

🥤

How bets are paid

Your symbol on 0 or 1 diethe house takes your bet
On 2 dicebet back + 2 × bet
On 3 dicebet back + 3 × bet
…up to 6 dicebet back + 6 × bet
🏦

Being the house (साहु बनौं)

The neighbors bet. Hold the shake button (or Space) to rattle the dice in the tumbler, then let go to slam it down. You collect every losing bet and pay every winner. Run out of sel roti and the house goes broke: टाट पल्टियो!

📈

Who wins in the long run?

With these rules the house keeps about 14 of every 100 sel roti bet, over time. That's why the host is always smiling. Try both sides and see.

Rules follow the common Nepali version (The Himalayan Times; Asanchowk). Some streets pay 1 matching die too.
