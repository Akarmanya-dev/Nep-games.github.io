कौडा

Kauda · Laxmi Puja night
  1. Four seats: तिया ३ (you), चौका ४, पन्जा ५, छक्का ६. The thrower (हाते) throws 16 cowries.
  2. Count the shells with the teeth up. 4 → चौका, 5 → पन्जा, 6 → छक्का, 7 → तिया, and round again.
  3. Thrower's own seat comes up: मार्यो! The thrower takes everything, throws again, and all bets double (मार-ताल).
  4. Another seat comes up: the thrower pays that seat, and the turn moves on.
  5. Tap any seat that isn't throwing to add a side bet (च्याखे). You can only stand up when the turn reaches चौका.

सेलरोटीको खेल · Play money only. We play with sel roti, never real money.

कसरी खेल्ने?

How to play

16 cowries

Each shell lands either teeth up (the slit shows) or teeth down (the smooth back shows). Only the teeth-up shells count.

Which seat wins

तिया ३3, 7, 11 or 15 shells up
चौका ४0, 4, 8, 12 or 16 (all one way counts for चौका)
पन्जा ५1, 5, 9 or 13
छक्का ६2, 6, 10 or 14

मार्यो and मार-ताल

Each turn, every seat except the thrower must bet the stake on its own seat. If the thrower's own seat comes up, the thrower takes everything on the mat and throws again. Everyone's bet then doubles: 1, 2, 4, 8… sel roti. That run of wins is the मार-ताल.

तिर्यो and the turn moves

If another seat comes up, the thrower pays that seat as much as its bet, and pays double on side bets placed there. Every other bet goes back to its owner. Then the turn passes anticlockwise to the next seat (दाउ सर्छ).

Your side bets (च्याखे)

When you're not throwing, tap any seat that isn't throwing to bet 1 sel roti on it. Tap again for more. A side bet pays double if that seat comes up and is lost if the thrower wins. ↺ फिर्ता takes your side bets back before the throw.

When you can leave

You can only stand up when the turn reaches चौका. Leave at any other time and कौडाले पिर्छ (the kauda curses you). After 24 throws the night ends at the next चौका turn. Finish with more sel roti than you started with!

Rules follow a Nepali rulebook (Nepali Nomad's Net, 2013) and Republica. Every family plays a little differently.
