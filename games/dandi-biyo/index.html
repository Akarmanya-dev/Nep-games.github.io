डन्डी बियो

Dandi Biyo
  1. Flick it out: hold, then let go, to flick the biyo out of the hole. A fielder catches it? आउट!
  2. Guard the hole: a fielder throws it back at the hole. Tap to hit it away. If it drops in, you're out and lose all your points.
  3. Score: tap to tip the biyo up. Quick taps = extra hits in the air. Hold and let go = the big hit.
  4. Distance from the hole is counted in dandi lengths for 1 hit, biyo lengths for 2, finger-spans for 3. Three misses in a row ends your innings.

Laptop: Space works the same as tapping / holding
