How to play

सावधान! · Attention

When PT sir shouts this, press and hold anywhere on the screen. You stand straight with your arms at your sides.

विश्राम! · At ease

Let go. You stand relaxed with your hands behind your back. You get about a second to react, less as the assembly goes on.

Stay balanced · सन्तुलन

At attention the hot sun makes you sway. Tilt your phone the way you want the bead to roll to bring it back to the green. The angle you hold the phone at when you press down counts as level. (Chose Slide on the start screen? Slide your finger instead.) If the bead hits the red end, you faint.

Watch PT sir

A yellow ! means he's about to turn around (sometimes he's bluffing). A red eye and red glow mean he's watching you. He only scolds what he sees: the wrong pose, or swaying out of the green.

Sneak a rest

When he's looking away you can let go and rest, even during सावधान! Just be holding again before he turns. Watch out for the fly on your nose and the poke from the kid next to you: both make you wobble.

Win and lose

Last until the bell (about a minute and a quarter). 3 scoldings and it's कान समातेर उठबस. Stay still while watched to be made मनिटर!
