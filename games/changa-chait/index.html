कसरी खेल्ने?

How to play

खिच · Hold to pull

Press and hold anywhere. Your kite darts the way its nose points.

ढिल · Let go to release

The kite spins slowly and drifts with the wind, sinking a little, and string runs out of your लट्टाई (spool).

Steering

Let go until the nose points where you want to go, then hold. That's how real fighter kites are flown.

Fights · चैट!

Cross a rival's string to fight. Two bars show how worn each string is (yours is yellow). The first string to wear through is cut: चैट!

Pick the right move

Higher than the other kite? Hold and pull. Lower? Let go and keep the string running out. Pulling from below almost always loses.

Stay up

Don't let your kite sink onto the rooftops and electric wires: तारमा अड्कियो! When your spool runs out, the kite can't go farther and letting go stops cutting. You have 3 kites.
